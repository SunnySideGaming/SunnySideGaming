Leveling Guide

Follow the ranges in order. Fill in where to hunt, what to fight, and what to prepare.

Level Map Atlas Monsters Notes
1 to 10 Novice Training Area Everything You can set your autoattack here and can level your novice up to level 30 here before Exp starts slowing down
10 to 30 Pay_fild01
Spores, Poporings You can start hunting Spore and Poporings for Strawberries and Grapes for Early SP supplies
Alternative
10 to 30		 Pay_dun00
Zombies, Skeletons, Familiar and Poporings Hunt Poporings for occasional Grapes for Early SP supplies and Zombies for Opals
30 to 50 Pay_fild02
Wolves, Snakes, Spores and Poporings You can stay here until you hit Job level 50.

Hunting Wolves, Snakes, Spores and Poporings can net you Strawberries which you can sell to get your Zenny for 2nd Job Change.
Alternative
30 to 40		 Pay_dun01
Eggyra, Archer Skeletons and Soldier Skeletons AOA and Card from Archer Skels, Stilettos and Card from Soldier Skel and Eggyra Card for SP Regen
Alternative
40 to 50		 Pay_dun02
Munak, Bongun, Hydras, Archer and Soldier Skeletons You can stay here until you hit Job level 50.

Hunt AOA and Card from Archer Skels, Stilettos and Card from Soldier Skel.
Kill the Hydras on the small pond for a chance for the Hydra Card

Beware from the single Nine Tails that spawns here.
