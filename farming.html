    Farming Spots

    Easiest spots

    Good for low levels and newer characters. Listed from easiest to harder.

    Easiest spots
    Location Key mobs Tips
    Comodo Field 01 Alligators, Blue Plant Lower-level intro map. Alligators are Brute/Water. Good for early players learning to manage aggro.
    Lighthalzen Field 01 Caramel, Metaling, Mole/Holden, Rafflesia, Stem Worm Low-mid level (23–36 classic). High-density Metaling spawns make it a fast early EXP spot and drops good items such as Large Jellopy and Rusty screws for Zenny
    Veins Field 04 Desert Wolf, Roween, Stapo Your Main Target here are Desert wolves. Play manually to drop Stilettos which can be sold for a good amount of zenny.
    Comodo Field 03 Toad, Mutant Dragonoid, Anolian, Pest Anolians are dense and easy to mob. Toads can drop SuNo Gear, while Mutant Dragonoid can drop good equipment
    Gonryun Field & Dungeon 01 Cloud Hermit, Peach Trees, Snakelord Zipper Bears and Peach Trees drop Royal Jelly and Honey at Dungeon 1F. Hunters are great here. Gonryun Field can spawn Zipper Bears and Baby Leopards are highly sought cards.

    Harder spots

    Better rewards, but tougher mobs or more prep needed. Listed from easier to hardest.

    Harder spots
    Location Key mobs Tips
    Geffen Dungeon F1 Hunter Flies and Poison Spores Your main targets are Hunter Flies and Poison Spores. You can farm Poison Spore (Required for ), Karvodainirol (Required for ) and their cards.
    Geffen Dungeon F2 Nightmares, Ghouls, Jakk, Tirfing and Ogretooth Nightmares can drop Blue Herbs, Blue Potions, Obb, Infiltrators and R.Ori. Ghouls Drops White herbs and R.Ori. Jakks can drop R.Elu and Tights. And ofcourse Tirfing and Ogretooth for a chance of their cards.
    Geffen Dungeon F3 Deviruchi, Nightmares, Marionettes and Whispers Etc loots from Deviruchi. Nightmare drops. Marionettes can drop Marionette Doll[1], Crystal Pumps and Brooch. Whispers for Fabrics and its ever elusive Card.
    Einbroch Field 05 Geographer, Demon Pungus, Mineral, Obsidian Desert wolf card and Peco Peco Egg Card works wonders here.
    Mineral and Obsidian drop Topaz/1-carat diamonds which sells for a pretty zeny. Demon Pungus drops Witch Starsand for Slim Whites. Focus on these 3 Small mobs. Great passive income.
    Niffleheim Field 01 Dullahan, Gibbet, Quve, Lude, Hylozoist Undead/Demon mobs → Holy and Fire are your best elements. Best to play Manually here as they can mob you very fast. Paladins, Star Gladiators, Knights are best used to farm here.
    Niffleheim Field 02 Dullahan, Gibbet, Quve, Lude, Loli Ruri, Bloody Murderer, Hylozoist Similar to Niffleheim Field 01 but these have harder hitting mobs such as Loli Ruri and Bloody Murderer. Best to play Manually here and with AGI Classes such as Assasins/Rogue with Aspersio
    Hugel Field 04 Novus, Deleter, Sleeper, Breeze Dragon and Formless mobs. Deleters drop useful cards; Sleeper can drop Great Natures. Breezes for Gust bows Carefull with Novuses as they can mobs you pretty fast here and possibly kill your Auto-Supports.
    Magma Dungeon F1 Kaho, Blazzer, Explosion High mob density + tons of loot. Bring Fire-element gear.
    Magma Dungeon F2 All High mob density + tons of loot, possible Infiltrator and card drops, Especially the Earth Deleter Card. Bring Fire-element gear, Holy/Water-Element weapons/Aspersio, Bring party if possible.
